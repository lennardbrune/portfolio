About

Friedrichshafen, Germany

I study Corporate Management & Economics at Zeppelin University. Before that I spent a year in Canada that I organised and paid for myself, laying decks on high-rises, framing houses and tending bar on weekends. Alongside my degree I teach myself programming and machine learning, with the aim of getting genuinely good at it.

What I enjoy most is finding the step in a process that costs time and replacing it with something that works better. At Phoenix Living that meant bringing Claude into the team, at Diehl Aviation it means building generative AI applications for internal processes. Right now I am most curious about robotics and what it takes for a company to actually put a robot to work.
